How It Works

Three simple steps to permanently secure your digital evidence.

1. Upload Evidence

Submit any digital file — documents, images, videos, audio. Our system accepts all formats and immediately begins cryptographic processing.

2. Hash & Record

A unique SHA-256 fingerprint is generated from your file's content and permanently written to an append-only blockchain block — creating an irrefutable timestamp.

3. Verify Integrity

At any point, re-hash the evidence file and compare it against the recorded hash. Any modification to the file — even a single byte — will be instantly detected.
